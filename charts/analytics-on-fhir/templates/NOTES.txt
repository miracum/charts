Thank you for installing {{ .Chart.Name }}.

A post-install/upgrade job loads synthetic FHIR data into the Pathling server and registers the resulting tables in the Hive Metastore.
Watch its progress with:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} get job,pods -l job-name={{ include "analytics-on-fhir.fullname" . }}-init-job
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs job/{{ include "analytics-on-fhir.fullname" . }}-init-job --all-containers --follow

If the job fails, it is kept (not deleted) so its logs remain available. It is retried at most {{ .Values.initJob.backoffLimit }} time(s) and terminated after {{ .Values.initJob.activeDeadlineSeconds }}s.

Access the Pathling FHIR server locally:

  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ $release }}-pathling-server 8080:8080
  curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/fhir/metadata
